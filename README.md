# Kavish Snaps Showcase

Build a complete, responsive website for my business based on the information below.

## 1. Business

**Business name:**  
Kavish Snaps

**What we do:**  
Kavish Snaps provides professional automotive photography for people and businesses selling cars online. We create high-quality photos that make vehicles look more appealing, professional and trustworthy in online listings.

**Target audience:**  
Private car sellers, dealerships and automotive businesses that sell cars online and want their listings to stand out from competing vehicles.

**Primary goal of the website:**  
Generate leads and bookings for automotive photography by showing potential customers the quality of the work and explaining why professional photography can improve the presentation of their car listing.

**Primary call to action:**  
Book a shoot

**Secondary call to action, if needed:**  
View portfolio

---

## 2. Brand & visual direction

**Brand personality:**  
Premium, automotive, modern, confident, cinematic, clean and professional.

The brand should feel like it understands cars and car culture while still being approachable to normal people who simply want to sell their vehicle.

Avoid making the brand feel like a generic photography studio, corporate dealership or luxury brand pretending to be exclusive.

**Colors:**  
Use a predominantly dark and neutral palette inspired by automotive photography.

Suggested direction:
- Near black: #0B0B0B
- Charcoal: #171717
- Off-white: #F5F5F2
- Medium grey: #A3A3A3

Use photography as the main source of color.

An accent color may be introduced later if required by the Kavish Snaps visual identity.

**Typography:**  
Use clean, modern sans-serif typography with strong headings.

Headings should feel bold, editorial and automotive without becoming futuristic or overly stylized.

Body text should remain simple and highly readable.

**Logo:**  
Use the existing Kavish Snaps logo if provided. Otherwise use a clean text-based “Kavish Snaps” wordmark as a temporary placeholder that can easily be replaced later.

**Visual references:**  
- https://gfwilliams.net/

What I specifically like about these references:

- GFWilliams: Large automotive imagery, dark visual direction, strong portfolio presentation, minimal interface, confident typography and a website where the photography itself becomes the main visual identity.
- GFWilliams: The site feels premium without relying on excessive effects, gradients or decorative elements.
- GFWilliams: Projects and automotive imagery are given enough space to create impact instead of being compressed into generic cards.

Use these references as inspiration for visual direction and UX principles. Do not simply copy their designs.

## 3. Content

Use the information I provide below as the source of truth.

**Main message/value proposition:**  
Make your car listing stand out.

Kavish Snaps creates professional automotive photography for cars being sold online, helping sellers present their vehicle at its best and create a stronger first impression with potential buyers.

The homepage should immediately communicate that Kavish Snaps photographs cars specifically with online selling and presentation in mind.

Possible hero copy:

“Make your car worth the click.”

Supporting copy:

“Professional automotive photography for cars being sold online. Give buyers a better first impression before they ever see the car in person.”

**Products/services:**

### Automotive listing photography
Professional exterior and interior photography designed specifically for online car listings.

The focus should be on:
- Showing the vehicle clearly
- Capturing flattering but realistic angles
- Creating a consistent professional presentation
- Highlighting important details
- Producing images suitable for FINN.no and other online marketplaces

### Automotive photography for dealerships
Photography for dealerships and automotive businesses that regularly need professional photos of vehicles for sale.

Use placeholder copy for exact dealership packages until more information is provided.

### Custom automotive shoots
For customers who want more creative, cinematic or enthusiast-focused automotive photography beyond a standard sales listing.

Only position this as a secondary service unless otherwise specified.

**Important information visitors need:**

Explain a simple process:

1. Get in touch / book a shoot
2. Choose a suitable location and time
3. Kavish Snaps photographs the exterior, interior and important details of the vehicle
4. The photos are edited and delivered ready for use in the customer's online listing

Exact pricing, number of photos, turnaround time, travel radius and location should use clearly labelled placeholders unless provided later.

Include placeholders for:

- Packages/pricing
- Number of delivered photos
- Delivery time
- Service area
- Booking availability

Do not invent these values.

**About the business:**  
Kavish Snaps specializes in photographing cars with a clear purpose: helping them look their best when presented online.

Rather than relying on quick phone photos or inconsistent listing images, Kavish Snaps creates a clean and professional visual presentation designed around the vehicle.

Use concise copy. The portfolio should communicate much of the quality of the service visually.

**Social proof:**  
Do not invent testimonials, number of cars photographed, dealership clients or statistics.

Create an optional testimonial/client section that should only appear if real content is supplied.

The portfolio itself should initially function as the strongest proof of quality.

**Contact information:**  
Use clearly labelled placeholders for:

- Phone number
- Email
- Instagram
- Location/service area

Make these easy to replace later.

## 4. Website structure

Create a clear information architecture based on the business goal and audience.

Use a relatively compact website because photography should do much of the selling.

### Navigation

Kavish Snaps logo

Links:
- Work
- Services
- About
- Contact

Primary navigation CTA:
- Book a shoot

Keep the navigation minimal.

### 1. Hero

Use one exceptional automotive photograph as the dominant visual.

Desktop may use either a large full-width photograph or near-full-screen layout.

Include:

Headline:
“Make your car worth the click.”

Supporting text:
“Professional automotive photography for cars being sold online.”

Primary CTA:
“Book a shoot”

Secondary CTA:
“View the work”

The visitor should immediately see what Kavish Snaps does without needing to scroll.

Avoid placing too much text over the photograph.

### 2. Selected work / portfolio

Make this one of the most visually prominent parts of the website.

Use large automotive photography rather than small repetitive cards.

Show a curated selection of cars.

Consider:
- Large editorial grid
- Alternating image sizes
- Full-width photographs between smaller image pairs
- Subtle image hover interactions on desktop

Images should feel like portfolio pieces rather than thumbnails.

Include:
“View all work” if a separate portfolio page is created.

### 3. Why professional photos matter

Connect the photography directly to the customer's goal of selling their vehicle.

Suggested heading:

“Your listing starts with the photo.”

Suggested copy:

“When buyers scroll through dozens of cars, your photos create the first impression. Professional images help your vehicle look clean, credible and worth taking a closer look at.”

Possible benefit points:

- Stand out among similar listings
- Present your car professionally
- Show important details clearly
- Create a stronger first impression
- Get photos ready for your online listing

Do not promise faster sales, higher selling prices or increased conversion unless evidence is provided.

### 4. Services

Show the core services simply.

Automotive listing photography  
Professional photos made for private and commercial car listings.

Dealership photography  
Consistent vehicle photography for businesses selling multiple cars.

Custom automotive shoots  
More creative photography for enthusiasts, brands and special vehicles.

Keep this visually simple and avoid generic three-card SaaS layouts.

### 5. How it works

Keep the process extremely easy to understand.

01 — Book  
Tell us about your car and arrange a suitable shoot.

02 — Shoot  
We photograph the exterior, interior and important details.

03 — Receive  
Get professionally edited images ready for your listing.

04 — List  
Upload the images to FINN.no or wherever you are selling your car.

### 6. Featured transformation / example listing

If suitable photography is available, show how Kavish Snaps transforms the presentation of an ordinary car listing.

This could eventually compare:
- Typical phone listing image
- Kavish Snaps photograph

Do not fabricate a before/after comparison.

If real comparison material is unavailable, omit this section from the first version.

### 7. About Kavish Snaps

Keep this short and personal.

Suggested direction:

“Cars are highly visual products, yet many are still sold using rushed photos that don't do justice to the vehicle. Kavish Snaps brings a clean, professional aesthetic to online automotive listings so every car makes the impact it deserves.”

### 8. Booking & Contact

Clean contact and booking inquiry form allowing clients to specify their car details, desired shoot type, and preferred timing, with clear placeholder contact details.
